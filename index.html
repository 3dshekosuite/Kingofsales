How is the price calculated?

Transfers and guide costs are divided by the number of travelers after EGP-to-USD conversion. Accommodation, domestic flights, tickets and lunches follow the same calculation model from your original app.

Egypt profit is added to Egypt operating costs only; other options and international flights are then added separately. Destination profit is calculated independently.
